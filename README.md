### Research

My PhD looks at federated learning (FL) on resource-constrained devices. I am building a
trustworthy FL framework in which a knowledge-driven agentic AI performs resource-aware
clustering and client selection, to improve system reliability and model quality across
heterogeneous networks.

Research interests:
- Federated learning on edge and embedded devices
- Knowledge representation (RDF/OWL, SPARQL) for trust assessment and client selection
- Agentic AI for decision-making in distributed systems
- Evaluating overhead, fault tolerance and robustness on physical test-beds

The PhD codebase is currently private. Please get in touch if you're interested in the work.
